## Pixabay terms

The Pixabay Content License forbids selling or distributing content "on a Standalone basis" (unchanged, as a file). Playing a track as
background inside a composed invitation counts as combining it with other media, so it is most likely allowed. To stay on the safe side:
only play these tracks inside an invitation, never offer them as downloads or on a public "music library" page, and don't market the
tracks themselves as a feature.
